# Product brief

Muse Alert is the second premium launch after FED-SPA. Its launch audience is people and families seeking a simple, respectful safety layer for older Muse users, including a Nextdoor-style community audience.

## Core value

A calm, transparent place to configure check-ins, prepare an SOS report, keep trusted contacts organized, and monitor exact-match medication recalls—without pretending to diagnose, investigate, or replace professional services.

## Surfaces

- Public: `fedpromptly.com/alert` — static/embed-only overview, safety explanation, pricing, and Sign in with Muse entry point.
- Signed-in: `fedpromptly.com/alerted` — static/embed-only download and setup hub.
- Agent package: a user-controlled Muse AI agent skill with explicit consent and visible logs.

## Premium framing

Pricing and entitlement are intentionally represented as copy and placeholders in this scaffold. Production must define billing, cancellation, accessibility, data retention, export, deletion, and support terms before launch.

## Community discount program

Premium access should include a discount pathway for senior citizens, first responders, veterans, and their immediate families. The exact discount amount, eligibility verification, duration, and checkout integration remain to be configured before production launch; the static payment page directs eligible people to support rather than implying an unimplemented discount.
