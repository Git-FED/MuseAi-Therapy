# Safety and escalation

Muse Alert is a coordination aid, not a clinical, legal, emergency, or investigative service.

## Escalation ladder

1. **Self-directed support:** show a clear next action, trusted contacts, and professional-contact recommendation.
2. **Family/trusted contact:** only after explicit opt-in, verified contact details, and a configured grace period.
3. **Non-emergency professional pathway:** only with membership authorization, a documented reason, and prior avenues attached.
4. **Emergency instruction:** when there may be immediate danger, instruct the user to contact local emergency services directly; do not delay for app workflows.
5. **Police pathway:** non-emergency only, final step, heavily disclosed, and never an investigation recommendation.

## Gates

Every automated or assisted escalation must pass: consent, identity/contact verification, context review, cooldown, duplicate prevention, human-readable preview, and audit logging. If any gate fails, stop and recommend a qualified professional or emergency service.

## FBI OFF LIMITS

No FBI referrals, submissions, threat intelligence, investigative claims, law-enforcement escalation, or implied federal reporting are supported by Muse Alert. The FBI is explicitly off limits in product logic and copy.

## High-risk situations

The product must not handle imminent self-harm, violence, abuse, medical crisis, missing-person emergencies, or other high-risk situations as a workflow. Tell the user to contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis/medical professional immediately.
