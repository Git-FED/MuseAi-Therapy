How it works

  1. Check-ins: opt-in daily, weekly, or hourly patterns; all are off by default.
  2. SOS: Muse Voice can prepare a factual report and point to local emergency services.
  3. Medication: exact drug-and-dose matching only, with pharmacist guidance.
  4. Escalation: family, non-emergency, and final police pathways require gates and prior avenues.

This page describes a scaffold. Production behavior requires consent, identity, security, accessibility, and professional review.
